## Embedded exercise data / worked examples

**BLUF email comparison** (new content, not in the Notes) — two full mock emails about an infrastructure consolidation ask:
- *Buried version*: 3 paragraphs of platform history/growth stats/competitor context before finally arriving at "Given all of this, I believe we should consider a 6-month infrastructure consolidation project before Q4" — annotated "← Ask buried here."
- *BLUF version*: Opens "I'm asking you to approve a 6-month infrastructure consolidation. It will reduce deployment complexity by 60% and unblock three roadmap items currently stalled" (annotated "← Ask in 1st sentence"), then states cost ($340K, one quarter reduced velocity), cost of not doing it (18 more months of bottleneck, $180K/yr overhead, retention risk), and pre-empts the expected objection ("Why now?" → three competitors consolidated in H1).

**Five Whys table** — same CPO/infrastructure-budget chain as the Notes, now in full table form (5 rows: stated objection → why not convinced → why doesn't it connect → why framed that way → why didn't we ask), ending at the same root cause (assumed we knew what the CPO cared about, we were wrong).

**Negotiation-sequence example quotes** (new, not in Notes) — concrete scenario language for each of the 4 steps: "I need the AI layer locked" vs. "I need 8 clean weeks of certainty on the schema" (Before); "It sounds like the renewal pressure is real..." (Opening); "I think we are disagreeing about whether field adoption or retention is the priority..." (During); "We both have legitimate constraints. What have we not considered yet?" (Stuck).
