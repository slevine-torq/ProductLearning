## Structure (29 sections, in order)
1. **Hero** ("Module 3") — title, subtitle, 4 waypoints (behaviors that define leadership / leading through performance / conditions for great work / two hands-on exercises).
2. **Class Expectations** — same 6 cohort norms as M1/M2.
3. **Syllabus** — 6-module grid, Module 3 marked "You are here."
4. **Agenda** — 4 waypoints for today.
5. **Section break 01** — "The Behaviors That Define Product Leadership."
6. **Q&A · What It Means to Lead People** (10 min) — 3 discussion prompts (best manager's one behavior; last time you gave uncomfortable feedback to a non-report; one IC habit you had to stop). Landing point: leaders default to avoidance or improvisation, both expensive; the module builds a system for the hard moments.
7. **Stop, Start, Continue** — 3-column framework: Stop (solving problems yourself, writing specs to prove understanding, being loudest voice, measuring by what you shipped, attending every meeting), Start (creating context, building PM judgment not giving answers, naming what good looks like, measuring by team output, protecting time), Continue (staying close enough to help, caring about outcome not just process, keeping product thinking sharp).
8. **The Player-Coach Dial** — visual spectrum: Over-Player (bottleneck) ↔ 4 modes (Direct/new person+task, Coach/growth edge, Support/complex ambiguity, Delegate/proven strength) ↔ Over-Coach (abdication). Takeaway: same PM can need Direct on a new problem and Delegate on their established domain.
9. **Self-Ownership Before Action** — 4 self-check questions before any feedback conversation (Have I been clear? / Let this go too long? / Is this about their behavior or my decision? / Am I helping them or relieving my own discomfort?), each with a 1-2 sentence elaboration.
10. **Section break 02** — "Leading People Through Performance."
11. **When Things Go Wrong · Diagnosis** — 4-card grid: Feedback problem (SBI is the tool), Clarity problem, System problem, Hiring mismatch — each with a 1-2 sentence definition. Warning callout: most "feedback problems" are actually clarity or system problems in disguise.
12. **Common Feedback Pitfalls + SBI** — 2-card grid (Withholding vs. Blurting, each with 3 consequence bullets), then the SBI formula card: Situation (specific, recent) → Behavior (observed not interpreted) → Impact (team/product/relationship) → Question (open, not directive). Worked example: the Tuesday-planning-session scope-change scenario (same example as the Notes), annotated inline with (S)/(B)/(I)/(Q) tags.
13. **A Clear Process for Any Performance Gap** — the 5-step numbered process (Real-time SBI feedback → State-of-the-relationship conversation → Documented shared understanding → N-day check-in → Formal PIP), each row tagged with its "most skipped/feared/avoided/delayed/last resort" characterization — matches the Notes' language.
14. **Supporting Your Team at Their Best** — 2-card grid (Provide recognition that lands; Support development that stretches) + takeaway callout on protecting the strongest performer (quietly absorbs the hardest problems, rarely raises a flag — watch energy shifts, shorter responses, less initiative).
15. **Lab · Diagnose a Team Situation** (15 min, individual) — 4 flow-steps: Describe the situation (1 paragraph, facts only) → Diagnose (four-type framework) → Write next action + first sentence of the conversation → Rehearse with AI (role-play prompt, 3 exchanges). Note: ICs without direct reports can apply the framework retrospectively to a situation they experienced. Links to `Module 3 - Lab Guide.html`.
16. **Quick Debrief** (2 min) — "Which diagnosis surprised you most: feedback, clarity, system, or hiring? Why?"
17. **Break** (5 min).
18. **Cameras On** reminder (photo-strip, no unique text).
19. **Section break 03** — "Creating the Conditions for Great Work."
20. **What Are You Optimizing For?** — 4-card grid of team structures (Outcome-Oriented/Spotify, Feature-Oriented/Amazon two-pizza, Platform+Product/Airbnb, Small Outcome Team/Linear), each with Optimises-for + Trade-off + real-world example — condensed-card version of the Notes' fuller paragraphs, same content and same 4 companies.
21. **Setting Expectations When Expanding Your Team** — the 5-dimension hiring-clarity framework (Direction/Growth stage/Market/Team/Org ambiguity) as a numbered skill-row list, matching the Notes almost verbatim including the Nikhil Singhal (Meta) source citation.
22. **Your Team Charter** — 4-card bad-vs-good comparison (What We Own / How We Decide / What Success Looks Like / How We Work), each showing a ✗ vague example and a ✓ specific example — same exact wording as the Notes' comparisons ("The core app experience" → "We own onboarding through Day 30...", etc.). Takeaway: "could a new PM on day one read this and know exactly what to do?"
23. **Section break 04** — "Hands-On Lab: Draft Your Team Charter."
24. **Lab · Draft Your Team Charter** (20 min, individual) — 3 flow-steps: Review the cross-team tension for your scenario (in Lab Guide) → Generate a first draft via the Lab Guide's AI prompt (feed in M1 strategy + M2 roadmap) → Refine until every line resolves a real decision/conflict. Links to `Module 3 - Lab Guide.html`.
25. **Show and Swap · Team Charter** (5 min, breakout) — same cold-read pattern as M1/M2 (1-minute silent read, no verbal context, then discuss where the charter leaves room for interpretation that could cause conflict).
26. **Key Takeaways** — 4 cards, same core points as the Notes.
27. **Extra Practice & Next Session** — 2 optional exercises (complete the full 4-part charter; run the self-check before your next hard conversation) + next-session preview (Module 4: Drive Alignment and Executive Influence) + deliverable-check reminder (add Team Charter to repo).
28. **Resources & Templates** — 2-card grid: Module 3 Lab Guide, On-Demand Content. Same lighter pattern as Module 2 (no dedicated interactive-builder tool).
29. **Q&A** — closing slide.
