# Module 1 · Glossary: Craft an Advanced Product Strategy

Key terms from the module, in plain language.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Product strategy** | A deliberate set of choices about where to play, how to win, and what to say no to, not a roadmap or a vision statement. |
| **Vision** | The aspirational, stable "why we exist": who we serve, what problem, why now. The *input* to strategy. |
| **Roadmap** | The specific products, features, milestones, and timelines that execute the strategy. |
| **Playing to Win** | The primary framework: a cascade of five linked choices, winning aspiration, where to play, how to win, capabilities required, management systems, that must cohere top to bottom. |
| **Winning aspiration** | What winning looks like in the *customer's* terms, not internal metrics. |
| **Where to play** | The chosen segment, geography, channel, and use case, including the deliberate no's. |
| **How to win** | The differentiator a competitor cannot easily replicate. |
| **Capabilities required** | What you must be world-class at to execute *how to win*; what you'll build, buy, or partner for. |
| **Management systems** | The metrics and rituals that reinforce your strategic choices so they don't decay into good intentions. |
| **Hard no** | One valuable thing you explicitly choose *not* to do, a deliberate trade-off that protects focus, not a list of deprioritized items. |
| **Rumelt's kernel** | A strategy's core: **diagnosis** (the real challenge), **guiding policy** (the overall approach), **coherent actions** (mutually reinforcing steps). |
| **Diagnosis** | An honest, specific read of the real challenge, uncomfortable enough to be useful. A softened diagnosis solves the wrong problem. |
| **Guiding policy** | The overall approach chosen to address the diagnosis. |
| **Coherent actions** | Initiatives that reinforce one another; you can't remove one without weakening the whole. |
| **Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)** | A lens on *winning aspiration*: the functional, social, and emotional progress a customer is "hiring" the product to make. |
| **Platform vs. Product** | A lens on *how to win*: whether you build a product that wins alone or a platform that makes others win with you. |
| **OKRs** | Objectives and Key Results, the bridge from strategy to execution: what we'll achieve and how we'll measure progress. |
| **Objective** | A qualitative, inspiring statement of what winning looks like in the period. |
| **Key Result (KR)** | A measurable **outcome** (not an output) that signals progress toward the objective. |
| **Outcome vs. output** | An outcome is a change in behavior or result (retention 34→62%); an output is a delivered thing (ship feature by Q2). |
| **Two-way / one-way door** | A reversible, low-cost decision vs. one that's hard or expensive to undo. Match your rigor to the door. |
| **Provisional bet + trigger** | Your best call given current information, plus the explicit signal that would make you revisit it. |
| **Kill criteria** | The specific metric, threshold, and date that would tell you a bet is no longer worth pursuing. |
| **AI co-pilot** | Using AI to monitor competitors, synthesize signal, and stress-test thinking, judgment stays with the leader. |
| **Strategy self-diagnostic** | Six checks: clear, names the real challenge, makes a hard bet, cascadable, coherent, committed. |
