## Header
StreamLine Spotlight, Product Roadmap · Q3 (current quarter) · Team: 2 engineers + 1 designer · Goal: increase 7-day activation.

## NOW (3-week sprint · the MVP core) — all Quick Win
| Feature | Rationale | Metric moved |
|---|---|---|
| A2 · "Why You'll Love This" Label | Hover-reveal AI reason a title matches taste. Directly attacks decision paralysis (the persona's core friction) at low build cost. | 7-day activation |
