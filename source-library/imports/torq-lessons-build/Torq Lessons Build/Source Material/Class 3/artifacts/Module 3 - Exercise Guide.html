Module 3 · Exercise Guide

Formulate your product hypothesis using data

Verify your Module 2 persona and friction points against three quantitative snapshots, Funnel, Cohort, and Segmentation, for your chosen scenario. Your goal is to review your qualitative research alongside these metrics to see where the data confirms, contradicts, or adds nuance to your earlier findings, ultimately evolving your Module 1 Problem Hook into a formal, data-backed product hypothesis.

💡 The reflection is the exercise. Work through all three snapshots for your scenario in order. After each one, answer the reflection before moving on, don’t skip ahead to the hypothesis. If the data contradicts your initial hunch, that’s a win: pivot your focus to where the actual metric leak is happening.

🎯 Work only on your chosen track

Use the scenario you committed to in Modules 1 & 2, keep building in the same world.

Scenario A · B2C streaming

StreamLine Spotlight

15,000+ titles built on volume; engagement is plateauing and churn rising as curated competitors gain share. Your initiative is StreamLine Spotlight: a premium curated space within the app.

Scenario B · B2B logistics

RouteLogic Velocity

An all-in-one fleet suite whose expanding scope created a gap between admin power and frontline utility. Your initiative is RouteLogic Velocity: reclaiming operational simplicity for daily users.

Your hypothesis statement

Based on [your qual + quant evidence], I believe that [solving X] for [persona] will result in [outcome], as measured by a [X%] change in [success metric]. I will protect [guardrail metric] and will make a go/no-go decision after [decision window].
