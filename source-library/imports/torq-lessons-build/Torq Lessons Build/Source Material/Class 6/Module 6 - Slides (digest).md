## Four launch case studies (verdict format — richer than Notes.md's prose version)

| Launch | Verdict | What happened | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| 📱 Apple iPhone | Success | The announcement *was* the launch — demand created 6 months pre-ship by controlling narrative/moment/message | A Big Bang launch done right defines a category |
| 💬 ChatGPT | Success | No paid media, no formal GTM, no launch event — PMF + organic sharing did everything (1M users/5 days) | When PMF is real, the product launches itself; GTM amplifies, doesn't substitute |
| 🥤 New Coke | Flop | Changed a winning formula without understanding what users valued emotionally, not just tasted | Never change what users love without understanding why — data shows what users *do*, not *feel* |
| 👓 Google Glass | Flop | Technology worked; GTM didn't — wrong audience, wrong price, no everyday use case | No GTM plan saves a product without PMF; launching harder isn't the answer |

## Goal → Audience → PM action table (fuller than Notes.md)

| Goal | Audience | PM action |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | New prospects, adjacent users, new regional/industry segments | Define the narrative; ensure marketing message matches product reality |
| **Engagement** | Non-adopters, lapsed users, or active users ready to go deeper | Monitor activation rates; identify friction blocking the aha moment |
| **Conversion** | Trial users ready to upgrade, upsell-ready customers, at-risk accounts | Brief sales on the target customer; set trial-to-paid benchmarks upfront |

## The GTM Lab ("Craft your GTM launch plan", 20 min)
5-step flow: (1) open M4 PRD feature + M5 experiment — don't introduce a new feature, (2) set goal & audience, (3) size the launch tier honestly, (4) pick top 3 channels (owned/earned/paid) + assign owner & budget to each, (5) set a phased timeline (beta→launch→post-launch) + success metrics including the "bad signal" that would mean it's not working. Deliverable: becomes the GTM slide of the final deck.
