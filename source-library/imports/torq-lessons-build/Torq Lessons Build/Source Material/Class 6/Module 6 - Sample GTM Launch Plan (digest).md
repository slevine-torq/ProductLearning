# Module 6 · Sample GTM Launch Plan (digest): PulseAI Adaptive Coach

**Format:** Static reference page — a fully worked example of the GTM Launch Plan Builder output. Reuses the "PulseAI" product persona seen in Module 4's Sample PRD (the adaptive fitness companion), giving the course a continuous worked example across modules.

## Header
Scenario: net-new product · Goal: Awareness · Tier: L · Status: Ready to launch · Feeds: final-project deck

## 1 · Feature & goal
- **Feature:** PulseAI Adaptive Coach — "the first fitness companion that adapts to your life instead of demanding you adapt to it."
- **Goal: Awareness.** Net-new product in a crowded fitness market. Priority is establishing perception — not another rigid workout app, but "the first fitness companion that actually listens."

## 2 · Target audience
- **Primary:** Fitness-curious professionals aged 25-40 who tried and abandoned traditional fitness apps due to guilt, rigidity, or lack of time. Active on social, responsive to empathetic, human-centered messaging.
- **Secondary:** Health & wellness creators/micro-influencers who speak to the "realistic fitness" niche and can amplify organically.

## 3 · Launch tier
> **Tier: L (multi-channel).** A net-new product entering a crowded market without an established user base. Requires proactive communication across multiple channels and a focused GTM plan to cut through the noise. A full-scale XL campaign isn't yet warranted, but a targeted multi-channel approach is essential to build early awareness and drive initial adoption.

## 4 · Channels (all 3 slots used, one of each type)
- **Owned:** Email & in-app onboarding sequences — reach users who already downloaded the app; activate them into their first coaching session.
- **Earned:** Micro-influencer seeding & social mentions — the empathetic angle is inherently shareable ("the app that doesn't guilt you"); targeting realistic-fitness creators maximizes earned reach.
- **Paid:** Paid social on Instagram & TikTok — audience is highly active on both; responds to short-form video demonstrating the experience rather than describing it.

## 5 · Enablement & assets
- Launch email to waitlist/early-access users
- In-app onboarding sequence introducing the adaptive coaching flow
- One-pager for influencer partners
- Short-form demo video of a real coaching interaction (for paid social)
- Updated app-store screenshots/description reflecting the adaptive-coaching angle

## 6 · Ownership & budget

| Owner | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| PM | Owns launch plan, coordinates all teams, monitors post-launch metrics |
| Marketing | Owns influencer outreach, paid-social creative, launch email. **Budget required** for paid-social activation + influencer-seeding fees |
| Design | Owns demo video, social visuals, app-store assets |
| Engineering | Owns app-store submission + launch-day performance monitoring |
| Customer Support | Briefed on coaching logic pre-launch. No additional budget |

## 7 · Timeline
- **Week 1:** Influencer seeding + early-access waitlist outreach
- **Week 2:** App-store submission; in-app onboarding goes live
- **Week 3:** Launch day — paid social activates, launch email sent
- **Week 4+:** Monitor metrics, collect feedback, assess iteration decision

## 8 · Success metrics
- **New user signups in first 30 days** — measures whether the awareness goal drives top-of-funnel growth
- **Time to first key action** — how fast new users reach their first coaching interaction (the core product moment)
- **Feature adoption rate** — % of users completing ≥1 adaptive workout session in week one

**Bad signal to watch for:** high reach + low signups → message-market mismatch (fix messaging, not channel). Strong signups + slow time-to-first-action → onboarding failure.

## Why this plan is "launch-ready" (explicit callout — the rubric)
> "The audience follows directly from the awareness goal, the tier is honestly sized for a net-new entrant, each channel reaches the audience where it already is, every activity has a named owner with budget surfaced up front, and the metrics match the goal, with a bad signal the PM can act on."
