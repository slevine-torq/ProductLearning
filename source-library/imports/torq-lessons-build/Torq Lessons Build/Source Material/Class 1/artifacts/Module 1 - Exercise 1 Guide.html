Module 1 · Exercise 1 Guide

Build a strategic discovery map

Run a sequence of three prompts in any AI tool (Figma AI, Claude, ChatGPT…) to build a product mindmap that evolves from generic noise into a viable strategic framework. Run each prompt in its own fresh tab/chat so the AI doesn’t build on old context. The goal is to find the failure points of AI-generated maps, and see where your judgment makes the output useful.

Exercise 1 · 15 min

Step 1 · Generate the generic baseline

Prompt 1 · shallowCreate a mindmap for a water tracking app.
Write one feature from the map that, if an engineer asked “why is this a priority for our 12-week roadmap?”, you honestly could not give a strategic answer for.✍️ Your answer…
💡 Remember: if you can’t justify a feature with a specific customer need or business goal, you’re just passing along AI-generated noise. That’s your first feature-factory trap.

Step 2 · Execute a structural upgrade

Prompt 2 · structuredYou are a product manager. Create a mindmap for a mobile app that helps people track daily water intake. Include features, user needs, and potential problems.
Write one feature that looks “correct” for the app but has zero strategic weight, you have no idea if it helps the company make money or whether a single user actually asked for it.✍️ Your answer…
💡 Remember: organization isn’t strategy. Just because the AI can create groups doesn’t mean it knows what’s a good use of engineering’s time.

Step 3 · Execute the final polish

Prompt 3 · strategicYou are a senior product manager at a health-tech startup preparing for a discovery sprint. Create a structured brainstorm mindmap for a mobile water-tracking app targeting busy adults aged 25 to 40 who know they should drink more but forget. Organize branches around: (1) core user jobs-to-be-done, (2) key friction points in existing behavior, (3) feature ideas ranked by effort vs. impact, (4) monetization paths with tradeoffs, (5) retention risks and how to address them. Flag any area where there's a real strategic tension to debate.
Compare V3 to your Step 1 baseline. What changed, and is the output objectively better, did the AI get smarter, or did your added PM knowledge (JTBD, effort/impact, retention) force it to be useful?✍️ Your answer…
Key takeaway: how does this prove the PM’s role is to think while the AI’s role is to execute?✍️ Your answer…
Take screenshots of your V1 vs. V3 maps and post them in #cohort-channel with your one-line takeaway.
