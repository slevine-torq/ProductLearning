Step 1 · Identify your persona

Define the specific user you’re designing for. Use your Problem Hook from Module 1 to filter the evidence in your Executive Summary so you’re solving the right business crisis. Paste this prompt into your Claude conversation:

Prompt · generate personasReview my Problem Hook: [Paste your Module 1 Problem Hook above] Based on that hook, consult our Executive Summary (specifically the user interviews and bug reports). Identify one or two distinct Personas who best embody the human struggle described in my hook. For each, provide: - Role: who they are, in one sentence. - Goal: what they want to achieve, in one sentence. - Friction: the specific 'Moment of Misery' from our research that is stopping them.

Step 2 · Document the current workaround

Identify exactly how your persona is “hacking” a solution today, this defines the competitive baseline your initiative must beat. Remember: your real competition is the status quo: the messy spreadsheets and manual habits users stick with to avoid the friction of learning something new.

Prompt · find the workaroundFor my persona, [your Step 1 Role] (goal: [your Step 1 Goal]), identify the specific external tools or manual processes they use to bypass their friction: [your Step 1 Friction]. Referencing our Problem Hook and the Executive Summary, list the specific steps they take and explain why this workaround is inefficient. Highlight how this 'hack' directly contributes to the business crisis we identified.
Auto-fills the Role, Goal & Friction you wrote in Step 1.

Step 3 · Design your future-state journey map

Flip the “before” state into a future-state workflow. Map how your initiative replaces those manual, external hacks to deliver the value proposition you defined in Module 1. Choose the strategy block that matches your project:

Scenario A · StreamLine Spotlight

Restore & curate

Restore brand authority and win back users by prioritizing human-led curation and restoration over mass-market scale.

Scenario B · RouteLogic Velocity

Strip & accelerate

Reclaim operational simplicity by stripping away legacy noise and ensuring the tool is the fastest for everyday work.

Pick the strategy block that matches your project. Your persona, strategy, and value prop all drop into the prompt below:

Prompt · future-state journey mapUsing the Persona and Workaround we identified, map a 4-stage 'Future State' journey for my initiative. Persona: [your Step 1 Role], goal: [your Step 1 Goal]; friction: [your Step 1 Friction]. The Strategy: [Pick a strategy block above] My Value Prop: [Paste your Module 1 Value Prop above] Output Requirements: - A one-page visual timeline. - A numbered list for the 4 stages. - Use an 'Action -> Benefit' format (max 15 words per bullet). - Each stage must include: Stage Name, User Action, Internal State, and Pain Point Addressed. - End with 3 Competitive Advantages over the manual workaround.
