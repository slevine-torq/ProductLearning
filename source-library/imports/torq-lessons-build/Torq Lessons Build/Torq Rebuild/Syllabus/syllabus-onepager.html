Prepared for: Director & Product Leadership Review
Status: Confidential — Internal Only
Date: August 2026

Most PM certifications train for a job Torq doesn't do. This program trains for the job we actually do — rebuilt clean-room from Product School's PMC certification, weighted 80/20 toward the execution work our consultants are staffed to deliver, and mechanically verified against the source material it's adapted from.

The six courses

CourseTasks / Art.Focus
1Strategic Product Thinking5 / 5PM role on a cross-functional team, AI output into strategy, lifecycle, problem framing.
2Discovering Product Opportunities5 / 5Reading a client's existing research, raw signal into insight, the status quo as real competitor.
3Analytics & Metrics for Product Decisions5 / 5Choosing a metric that matters, the AARRR funnel, slicing/sizing, a defensible hypothesis.
4High-Velocity Product Roadmaps6 / 6Roadmaps, MVP/MoSCoW scoping, a PRD an engineer can build from, acceptance criteria & QA.

6Go-to-Market Launch Plans (capstone)7 / 7Goals/tiers/channels, enablement & budget, the post-launch call, a deck assembling all six courses.
