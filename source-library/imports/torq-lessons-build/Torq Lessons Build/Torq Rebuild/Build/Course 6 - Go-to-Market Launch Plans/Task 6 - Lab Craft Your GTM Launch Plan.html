Build the plan, step by step

  1. Set the goal and audience

    From Task 2 — what is this launch actually for, and for whom specifically?

  2. Pick the tier and channels

    Size the moment, then pick where it travels — owned, earned, paid, or a mix.

  3. Confirm enablement is real, not assumed

    From Task 3 — what does each team actually need before this goes live?

  4. Bring in what you already validated

    This is where Courses 4 and 5 pay off directly — don't restate them, cite them.

  5. Name the post-launch metric and decision plan

    From Task 5 — which funnel stage you'll watch, and what would trigger each of the four calls.
