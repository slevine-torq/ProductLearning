## Task 1 — Why Launches Fail and What a Plan Actually Does

### Block: Launch Checklist Key Terms — Type: Card flipping

1. Term: Launch goal & audience | Definition: What this specific launch needs to accomplish, and for whom — not "everyone."
2. Term: Launch tier | Definition: How big a moment a launch deserves — S/M/L/XL, sized like any other piece of scoped work.
3. Term: Enablement | Definition: What Sales, Support, and Customer Success need before day one, not after the first ticket.
4. Term: Success metrics | Definition: How you'll judge whether the launch worked, tied back to the goal set first.

### Block: Landed or Stumbled — Type: Card classification

**Buckets:** Landed, Stumbled

1. "The launch moment and the product told the same story." → Landed
2. "Strong technology, but the go-to-market didn't account for how it would land publicly." → Stumbled
3. "Removed nearly every barrier between the product and someone trying it." → Landed
4. "Good data behind the change, but it triggered a backlash strong enough to force a reversal." → Stumbled

## Task 2 — Goals, Tiers, and Channels

### Block: Channel Key Terms — Type: Card flipping

1. Term: Owned channel | Definition: Fully controlled — in-app messaging, email list, blog. No per-message cost, limited reach.
2. Term: Earned channel | Definition: Attention you didn't pay for — press, word of mouth, organic sharing. Highest credibility, least controllable.
3. Term: Paid channel | Definition: Advertising and sponsored placement. Fastest to scale, stops when the budget does.
4. Term: Launch vs. release | Definition: A release can ship silently to a segment; a launch is a deliberate choice to make noise about it.

### Block: Match the Goal — Type: Card classification

**Buckets:** Awareness, Engagement, Conversion

1. "People who don't know this exists yet." → Awareness
2. "People who've seen it but haven't tried it." → Engagement
3. "People actively evaluating it, close to a decision." → Conversion

## Task 5 — Launch Phases and the Post-Launch Call

### Block: Post-Launch Key Terms — Type: Card flipping

1. Term: Beta / early access | Definition: A small group gets it first, under real conditions — catches what a controlled experiment couldn't.
2. Term: Double-down | Definition: Working as hoped — commit more resourcing behind it.
3. Term: Pivot | Definition: Eric Ries's term — the core idea holds, the execution doesn't, so you change direction while keeping what you validated.
4. Term: Deprioritize | Definition: No real signal after a genuine test — redirect effort elsewhere.

### Block: Which Metric to Watch — Type: Card classification

**Buckets:** Acquisition, Activation, Revenue / Referral

1. "Our goal was awareness." → Acquisition
2. "Our goal was engagement." → Activation
3. "Our goal was conversion." → Revenue / Referral
