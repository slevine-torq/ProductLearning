# Product Leadership prompt catalog

Internal-use finding aid. Product Leadership raises familiar PM work to executive altitude: strategy choices, influence, organizational design, financial judgment, and repeatable leadership behavior.

## PL-01 — Skeptical CSO strategy pressure test

- Type: Copy-ready AI prompt
- Fidelity: **Verbatim capture**
- Sources: [M1 slides](<../source-library/imports/product-leadership/Product Leadership/Source Material/Class 1/artifacts/Module 1 - Slides (digest).md>), [M1 lab](<../source-library/imports/product-leadership/Product Leadership/Source Material/Class 1/artifacts/Module 1 - Lab Guide (digest).md>), and dynamic [Strategy Sprint Builder](<../source-library/imports/product-leadership/Product Leadership/Source Material/Class 1/artifacts/Module 1 - Strategy Sprint Builder (digest).md>)

```text
You are a skeptical Chief Strategy Officer reviewing a product strategy draft. Your job is to find where it is soft, vague, or likely to fail, not to validate it.

Here is the strategy:
[PASTE YOUR CASCADE, HARD NO, AND OKRs HERE]

Please:
1. Identify the single biggest assumption I am making that could be wrong.
2. Ask me the one question a board member would ask that I cannot yet answer.
3. Tell me if any of my KRs are actually outputs disguised as outcomes.
4. Point out one thing I said I would not do and make the case for reconsidering.
5. In one sentence: is this a strategy or a wish list? Why?

If your response feels too gentle, add: "Now be harsher: what would a competitor's strategy team say?"
```

The builder interpolates learner content, but it is an occurrence of this same pressure-test pattern rather than a separate prompt.

## Presentation timing

The source contains two valid presentation formats. Use **2–3 minutes per learner when everyone presents**; use **about 7 minutes when a smaller volunteer group showcases**. This is a delivery-setting choice, not a prompt contradiction.
